Not yet wired — surfaced for transparency
·
Explicit pause / resume events. HLS players don't beacon those without active SDK instrumentation. We infer drop-off from chunks ceasing — fine for retention, but can't distinguish "paused at 20:00" from "closed the tab at 20:00".
·
Ad chunk tracking. track=ad_audio/ad_video are reserved values; the stitcher's RouteAdStitchAudioSplit carries a TODO. Ad-impression analytics still come from the existing ad_analytics / VAST pixel path.
·
Closed captions / chapter markers. Not modelled.
·
Per-frame quality switches inside one rendition. We see which rendition was served, not adaptive-bitrate fluctuations within it.
·
Thumbnail strip on scrub-heatmap. Pixel says we already have IFRAME-track thumbnails (~6fps). Wiring them to the heatmap is a follow-up — would let editorial preview the exact frame at each hot spot.
